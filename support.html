一、三款应用简介

SNTWET 行情研究 · com.senwit.mobile

一款市场数据查阅与技术分析研究工具。提供自选列表与 K 线图表、每日市场简报、按技术条件筛查、板块与商品／宏观面板。所有数据来自公开的第三方财经数据服务，均为延时数据。

它不是什么：不连接任何券商或交易所账户，不能下单、不能划转资金，不托管任何资金，也不提供个性化投资建议。“分析”页中的市值与盈亏计算，其输入数字全部由您自己手动键入，应用只做算术并显示结果。

支持系统：iOS 15.0 及以上 · 界面语言：简体中文 · 无需注册

SNTWET 离线翻译 · com.shenzhenbo.OfflineTranslator

相机取景翻译、照片文字翻译、文本翻译与语音翻译，内置 1117 条工程机械与 IT 专业术语对照表。翻译与文字识别在设备上完成。

请先下载语言包：本应用使用 iOS 系统提供的翻译能力，首次使用某个语言对时需要联网下载一次语言包（在应用内“设置”页一键下载）。下载完成后即可离线使用。

支持系统：iOS 18.0 及以上 · 界面语言：简体中文 · 无需注册

SNTWET 语言训练 · com.senwit.training

面向工程机械行业的职业英语训练：分阶段词卡、例句发音、单元测验、错题本、收藏夹与行业词典。课程内容与 457 个发音音频全部随应用安装，完全离线可用。

支持系统：iOS 18.0 及以上 · 界面语言：简体中文 · 无需注册 · 无需联网

二、常见问题

通用

需要注册账号吗？

三款应用都不需要注册，也没有登录界面。您的数据只保存在自己的设备上。

数据会上传吗？会看到我的隐私吗？

三款应用都不设服务器、不收集身份信息、无广告、无追踪。各自的具体数据流向请看下方“隐私政策”一节，每一条都写明了。

换手机后进度还在吗？

数据保存在设备本地，删除应用或更换设备后不会自动迁移。这是“不上传任何数据”的必然代价。

怎么彻底删除我的数据？

长按主屏幕上的应用图标 → 删除 App。本机数据随之全部清除；开发者处本来就没有您的任何数据。

SNTWET 行情研究

行情是实时的吗？

不是。本应用使用的是公开财经服务提供的延时数据，不是交易所实时授权行情，延迟时长由数据提供方决定。请不要把它当作实时报价使用。

某个板块／某只标的显示“数据暂不可用”怎么办？

说明该数据源当次请求失败（网络问题，或对方接口临时变更／限流）。应用的设计是明确显示取不到，而不是用别的数字顶替。请稍后重试；若长期不恢复，请发邮件告诉我们具体标的与页面。

“分析”页的盈亏是从哪来的？

完全来自您自己键入的股数与成本价。应用不连接任何券商，无法读取您的真实账户，也不会核对这些数字。它只是一个在本机运行的计算器。

能帮我买卖股票吗？能告诉我买什么吗？

不能，也不会。本应用没有任何下单能力，也不提供投资建议或推荐。所有内容仅供研究参考，任何投资决策及其后果由您自行承担。

SNTWET 离线翻译

为什么第一次用要联网？

翻译能力由 iOS 系统提供，语言包需要由系统下载一次。请到应用内“设置”页选择语言对并点“下载或更新所选语言包”。下载完成后即可断网使用。

相机是黑的／提示需要权限

请到 iOS「设置 > 隐私与安全性 > 相机」中允许本应用。若您不想给相机权限，仍可使用底部的“文字”与“语音”翻译，以及从相册选图翻译。

语音识别不出来

请确认已允许“麦克风”和“语音识别”权限。部分语言需要您先在「设置 > 通用 > 键盘 > 听写」中添加对应的听写语言。

翻译会把我的文字发到网上吗？

不会。翻译全部在设备上完成，应用内没有任何把待翻译内容发往第三方翻译服务的功能。唯一需要联网的只有一次性的语言包下载，而那是由 iOS 系统完成的。

SNTWET 语言训练

发音需要联网吗？

不需要。发音音频随应用一起安装在设备里。

为什么个别词条的发音音色和别的不一样？

极少数词条没有预录音频，会退回使用 iOS 系统的朗读功能，音色因此不同。我们会在后续版本补齐。

会录我的声音吗？

不会。本应用只有“播放发音”，没有录音功能，也不申请麦克风权限。

1. The three apps

SNTWET Market Research · com.senwit.mobile

A market-data reading and technical-analysis research tool: watchlists and candlestick charts, a daily market brief, technical screening, plus sector, commodity and macro panels. All data comes from public third-party financial data services and is delayed.

What it is not: it connects to no brokerage or exchange account, cannot place orders or move funds, custodies nothing, and offers no personalised investment advice. In the Analysis tab, every input figure is typed in by you; the app only performs arithmetic and displays the result.

Requires iOS 15.0+ · Interface language: Simplified Chinese · No sign-up

SNTWET Offline Translator · com.shenzhenbo.OfflineTranslator

Live camera translation, photo text translation, text translation and voice translation, with a built-in glossary of 1,117 construction-machinery and IT terms. Translation and text recognition run on device.

Download a language pack first: the app uses the translation capability provided by iOS, so the first use of a language pair requires a one-time online download of the language pack (one tap in the app's Settings screen). After that it works offline.

Requires iOS 18.0+ · Interface language: Simplified Chinese · No sign-up

SNTWET Language Training · com.senwit.training

Vocational English training for the construction-machinery industry: staged flashcards, sentence pronunciation, unit quizzes, a mistakes notebook, bookmarks and an industry dictionary. All lesson content and 457 pronunciation recordings ship inside the app — fully usable offline.

Requires iOS 18.0+ · Interface language: Simplified Chinese · No sign-up · No network needed

2. FAQ

General

Do I need an account?

No. None of the three apps has registration or a login screen. Your data stays on your own device.

Is anything uploaded? Can you see my data?

None of the apps runs a server, collects identity data, shows ads or does tracking. Each app's exact data flow is spelled out in its privacy policy, linked below.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Data is stored locally, so it does not transfer automatically when you delete the app or switch devices. That is the unavoidable cost of uploading nothing.

How do I delete my data?

Press and hold the app icon on the Home Screen and delete the app. All local data goes with it; the developer never had any of it.

SNTWET Market Research

Are quotes real-time?

No. The app uses delayed data from public financial services, not exchange-licensed real-time feeds. The delay is determined by each provider. Please do not treat it as a real-time quote.

A panel says data is unavailable — what should I do?

That request to the data provider failed (network issue, or the provider changed or throttled its endpoint). The app is deliberately designed to state clearly that data could not be retrieved rather than substitute some other number. Please retry later; if it persists, email us with the symbol and screen.

Where do the profit/loss numbers in the Analysis tab come from?

Entirely from the share count and cost basis you type in. The app connects to no broker, cannot read any real account, and does not verify those numbers. It is a calculator running on your phone.

Can it trade for me, or tell me what to buy?

No, and it will not. The app has no order-placement capability and gives no investment advice or recommendations. Everything is for research reference only; all investment decisions and their consequences are yours.

SNTWET Offline Translator

Why does the first use need internet?

Translation is provided by iOS, and the language pack must be downloaded once by the system. Open Settings inside the app, pick your language pair and tap "Download or update the selected language pack". After that it works offline.

The camera is black or asks for permission

Allow the app in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera. If you prefer not to grant camera access, the Text and Voice translation modes at the bottom still work, as does translating an image picked from your photo library.

Voice input is not recognised

Make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed. Some languages also require you to add the matching dictation language under Settings > General > Keyboard > Dictation.

Is my text sent over the internet to be translated?

No. Translation happens entirely on the device; the app has no feature that sends translatable content to a third-party translation service. The only thing that needs the internet is the one-time language-pack download, and that is performed by iOS.

SNTWET Language Training

Does pronunciation need internet?

No. The audio files are installed with the app.

Why do a few entries sound different?

A small number of entries have no pre-recorded audio and fall back to the iOS text-to-speech voice. We will fill these in in a future update.

Does it record my voice?

No. The app only plays pronunciation. It has no recording feature and requests no microphone permission.

3. Feedback and bug reports

Feature suggestions are welcome too — please describe the problem you want solved, not only the button you want added.
