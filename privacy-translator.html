三、语音识别：什么时候在设备上，什么时候不在

本应用在开始识别前会检查所选语言是否支持设备端识别：

  • 若支持：应用会强制要求设备端识别（requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true），录音内容不会离开您的设备。
  • 若不支持该语言的设备端识别：识别将由 Apple 的系统语音识别服务完成，这意味着音频会按 Apple 的处理方式发送至 Apple 的服务器。此过程由 iOS 系统负责，本应用不接触、不留存这些音频，开发者也拿不到它。Apple 对语音识别数据的处理适用 Apple 自己的隐私政策。

是否支持设备端识别取决于语言、机型与您在系统“设置 > 通用 > 键盘 > 听写”中已下载的听写语言，本应用无法替您决定。

四、翻译引擎（全部在设备上运行）

本应用的翻译按以下顺序尝试，全部尝试完才返回结果：

  1. 内置专业术语库（随应用一起安装，1117 条工程机械与 IT 术语对照）。完全离线，不联网。
  2. Apple Intelligence 设备端语言模型（需 iOS 26 且机型与系统开关支持）。在设备上运行，不联网。
  3. iOS 系统翻译框架（Apple Translation）。下载语言包之后完全离线运行。语言包本身由 iOS 系统下载与管理，首次使用某个语言对时需要联网下载一次；下载动作由系统发起，本应用不经手，也没有自建的下载服务器。

没有第三方翻译服务。本应用不向任何第三方翻译服务发送您的文字。上述三档全部在您的设备上运行；应用中不存在把待翻译内容发往外部翻译接口的代码路径。

五、保存在您设备上的数据

本应用只在本地保存少量偏好设置（源语言与目标语言的选择），使用 iOS 的 NSUserDefaults。不保存翻译历史、不保存录音、不保存照片。删除应用即删除全部本地数据。

English

1. In one sentence

This app has no accounts, no registration and collects no personally identifiable information. It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking components. The developer runs no servers, so the developer receives none of your data. Camera frames, photos and translations are processed on your device and are never sent to a third-party translation service. The one case in which data can leave the device is the system speech-recognition fallback, which section 3 spells out plainly.

2. Permissions: what is requested and why

PermissionWhen requestedPurpose and limits
Camera When the main screen first opens (live camera translation is the app's home screen) Used for the live viewfinder and still capture, to recognise text in the frame. Text recognition runs on the device. Camera frames are never uploaded, never saved and never written to your photo library; they are discarded after translation.
Microphone Only when you open the Voice screen and press the record button Records the speech you want translated. The recording is used for that single transcription only; it is not saved as a file and never sent to the developer.
Speech Recognition Together with the microphone, only when you press record Converts your recording into text. See section 3 — not every language can be recognised on device.
Photos Not requested Choosing an image uses the system photo picker, which runs outside this app's process. The app cannot browse your library; it receives only the single image you pick, and never writes anything back to your library.

3. Speech recognition: when it stays on device, and when it does not

Before recognition starts, the app checks whether the selected language supports on-device recognition:

  • If it does, the app forces on-device recognition (requiresOnDeviceRecognition = true) and the audio never leaves your device.
  • If it does not, recognition is performed by Apple's system speech recognition service, which means audio is sent to Apple's servers as part of that system service. This is handled by iOS; the app neither touches nor retains that audio, and the developer never receives it. Apple's own privacy policy governs how Apple processes speech recognition data.

Whether on-device recognition is available depends on the language, the device model, and the dictation languages you have downloaded in Settings > General > Keyboard > Dictation. The app cannot change this on your behalf.

4. Translation engines (all on device)

Translation is attempted in the following order:

  1. Built-in terminology glossary (installed with the app; 1,117 machinery and IT term pairs). Fully offline.
  2. Apple Intelligence on-device language model (requires iOS 26 and a supported device with the feature enabled). Runs on device, no network.
  3. The iOS system translation framework (Apple Translation). Fully offline once the language pack is installed. Language packs are downloaded and managed by iOS itself, which requires a one-time internet connection the first time you use a language pair. That download is initiated by the system; the app does not handle it and there is no download server operated by the developer.

There is no third-party translation service. This app never sends your text to any third-party translation service. All three tiers above run on your device; no code path exists in the app that would send translatable content to an external translation endpoint.

5. Data stored on your device

The app stores only a couple of preferences locally (your source and target language selection) using NSUserDefaults. No translation history, no recordings and no photos are stored. Deleting the app removes all local data.

6. What this app does not do

  • No accounts, no registration, no collection of name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising SDK, analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK; the IDFA is never read.
  • No "Tracking" as defined by Apple's App Privacy framework.
  • No access to contacts, calendar, location or health data.
  • Your translations, recordings and photos are never sold, rented or handed to anyone.

7. Children's privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children — it collects identity information from no one.

8. Your rights and how to reach us

The developer holds none of your data, so there is no server-side record to access or delete; deleting the app erases everything locally. For any question, write to sntwet@gmail.com.
