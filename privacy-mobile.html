一、一句话概括

本应用不设账号、不要求注册、不收集任何可识别您身份的信息，也不包含任何广告或第三方追踪组件。您在应用内输入的全部内容只保存在您自己的设备上。唯一会离开设备的信息，是为了取得行情数据而必须发送给公开财经数据服务的股票／期货代码等查询参数——这一点在下文第三节逐条列明，不做任何隐瞒。

二、本应用不做的事

  • 不要求注册、不设登录、不采集手机号、邮箱、姓名或任何身份信息。
  • 不连接任何证券经纪商或交易所账户，不读取您的真实持仓，不下单、不划转资金。
  • 不申请相机、麦克风、通讯录、日历、照片、健康或定位权限。
  • 不集成任何广告 SDK、分析 SDK 或崩溃上报 SDK；不读取 IDFA／广告标识符。
  • 不进行 App Store 隐私定义中的“追踪（Tracking）”，即不会把您的数据与第三方数据关联用于定向广告。
  • 开发者不运营任何服务器，因此技术上不存在“开发者收到您的数据”这条路径。

三、会离开设备的信息（如实披露）

本应用的行情、财务、日历等数据全部直接向公开的第三方财经数据服务请求，不经过开发者的任何中转服务器。发起请求时，以下信息会随请求到达对方服务器：

  • 您查询或加入自选的证券代码、期货／商品代码（例如 AAPL、0700.HK、600519.SS、BTCUSDT）。这些代码会以明文出现在请求地址中。
  • 您设备的公网 IP 地址以及操作系统／网络栈自动附带的常规 HTTP 头。这是任何联网请求都不可避免的，本应用无法也不会去掉它。

请注意：由于这些是第三方运营的服务，开发者无法控制、也无法向您保证对方是否记录、保留或如何使用上述请求日志。如果您不希望自己的关注标的出现在第三方的访问日志中，请不要使用本应用。

四、保存在您设备上的数据

以下数据只写入 iOS 本地存储（NSUserDefaults / SharedPreferences），既不上传，也不备份到开发者处：

  • 您添加、删除或排序的自选证券代码列表。
  • 您在“分析”页手动输入的数值（股数、成本价等）。这些数字完全由您自行录入，应用不会去任何券商核对它们，也不会把它们发送到任何地方；它们只用于在本机做算术计算并显示结果。
  • 界面偏好设置（如市场切换、列宽、图表布局）。

删除本应用即会一并删除上述全部本地数据。您也可以在应用内逐条删除自选与手动录入的数值。

五、儿童隐私

本应用面向成年人的市场资讯阅读需求，不面向 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息（事实上本应用不收集任何人的身份信息）。

六、您的权利

由于开发者不持有您的任何数据，通常意义上的“查询／导出／删除我的数据”请求在本应用中没有对应的服务器端对象——所有数据都在您手里，删除应用即彻底删除。若您对本政策有疑问，或希望确认某项具体行为，请发邮件至 sntwet@gmail.com，开发者会在合理时间内答复。

七、重要声明：本应用不是投资顾问

本应用仅展示公开市场数据并在设备上进行技术指标计算。所有数据均为延时数据，非交易所实时授权行情，可能存在延迟、缺失或错误。应用内的任何数字、图表或文字都不构成投资建议、要约或推荐，也不代表对任何证券未来价格的预测。任何投资决策及其后果由您自行承担。

八、政策变更

本政策如有实质性变更，将更新本页顶部的“最近更新”日期，并在必要时于应用更新说明中提示。

English

1. In one sentence

This app has no user accounts, requires no registration, and collects no personally identifiable information. It contains no advertising and no third-party tracking components. Everything you enter stays on your own device. The only information that leaves your device is the ticker symbol you are looking up, which must be sent to public financial-data services in order to retrieve quotes. Section 3 lists every one of those services by name.

2. What this app does not do

  • No sign-up, no login, no collection of phone number, email, name or any identity data.
  • No connection to any brokerage or exchange account. It cannot read your real holdings, place orders, or move funds.
  • No access to camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, photos, health data or location.
  • No advertising SDK, analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK. The IDFA / advertising identifier is never read.
  • No "Tracking" as defined by Apple's App Privacy framework: your data is never linked with third-party data for targeted advertising.
  • The developer operates no servers, so there is no technical path by which your data could reach the developer.

3. What leaves the device (full disclosure)

All market, fundamental and calendar data is requested directly from public third-party financial data services. There is no relay server operated by the developer. When a request is made, the following reaches the third party's servers:

  • The security or commodity symbols you look up or add to your watchlist (for example AAPL, 0700.HK, 600519.SS, BTCUSDT). These appear in clear text in the request URL.
  • Your device's public IP address and the standard HTTP headers added by the operating system's network stack. This is unavoidable for any network request; the app neither can nor does strip it.

Please note: because these are services operated by third parties, the developer cannot control and cannot warrant whether, or for how long, those parties log or retain such requests. If you do not want the symbols you follow to appear in a third party's access logs, please do not use this app.

4. Data stored on your device

The following is written only to local iOS storage (NSUserDefaults). It is never uploaded and never backed up to the developer:

  • The watchlist symbols you add, remove or reorder.
  • Numbers you type into the Analysis tab (share count, cost basis). These figures are entered entirely by you; the app never verifies them against any broker and never transmits them anywhere. They are used solely for on-device arithmetic and display.
  • Interface preferences (market selection, column widths, chart layout).

Deleting the app deletes all of the above. You can also remove watchlist entries and typed figures individually inside the app.

5. Children's privacy

This app is intended for adults reading market information. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children — indeed it collects identity information from no one.

6. Your rights

Because the developer holds none of your data, the usual "access / export / delete my data" requests have no server-side object to act on: all data is in your hands, and deleting the app erases it completely. For any question about this policy, write to sntwet@gmail.com and the developer will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Important notice: this app is not an investment adviser

This app displays public market data and performs technical-indicator calculations on the device. All data is delayed; it is not exchange-licensed real-time data and may be late, incomplete or wrong. No number, chart or sentence in this app constitutes investment advice, an offer, or a recommendation, nor a prediction of any security's future price. All investment decisions and their consequences are yours alone.
