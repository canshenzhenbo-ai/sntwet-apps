一、一句话概括

本应用是一款完全离线的行业英语学习工具。全部课程文本与题库都随应用一起安装在您的设备上；单词与例句的发音由 iOS 系统内置的语音合成（朗读）在您的设备上实时生成，安装包内不含任何音频文件。本应用没有服务器、不设账号、不联网上传任何内容，您的学习记录只存在您自己的手机里。

二、完全离线：可核验的事实

  • 应用安装包内含全部课程数据与题库；发音由 iOS 系统内置的语音合成（朗读）在本机实时生成，安装包内不含任何音频文件。学习过程无需网络。
  • 应用的网络请求层是本地模拟实现，不含任何真实的网络调用（无 HTTP 请求、无 WebSocket）。
  • 应用界面由内嵌的 WKWebView 呈现，其内容通过自定义的本地资源协议从安装包内读取。该 WebView 只加载安装包内的本地内容——任何指向外部网址的跳转都不会在应用内打开；其中 http/https 链接（例如本隐私政策全文）会交给系统浏览器打开，其余协议一律拒绝。应用本身不提供浏览互联网的功能。
  • 应用不申请麦克风、相机、通讯录、日历、定位、照片或健康权限。

一处诚实的补充：界面框架（uni-app）自带的一段默认样式中，含有一条指向 cdn.dcloud.net.cn 的装饰性背景图引用。它是框架预置的样式残留，不携带任何用户数据；在没有网络时会静默失败，对功能没有任何影响。我们把它列出来，是因为“完全离线”这句话应当经得起抓包核对。

三、发音功能不需要麦克风

应用中的“发音／朗读”是播放声音，不是录音：全部发音都由 iOS 系统内置的语音合成（朗读）在您的设备上实时生成，安装包内不含预录音频文件。该功能只输出声音，不采集任何音频输入。因此本应用不申请、也不需要麦克风权限，不含跟读评测或录音比对功能。

四、保存在您设备上的数据

  • 学习进度、已学词卡、测验成绩与错题本。
  • 收藏夹内容。
  • 您自行填写的昵称等界面偏好（如该版本包含此功能）。

删除应用即删除全部上述数据。

English

1. In one sentence

This is a fully offline industry-English learning app. All lesson text and quiz content are installed on your device together with the app; pronunciation is generated on your device at playback time by the iOS built-in speech synthesis (text-to-speech) service, and the app bundle contains no audio files. There is no server, no account and nothing is uploaded; your learning records exist only on your own phone.

2. Fully offline: verifiable facts

  • The installed app bundle contains all course data and quiz content; pronunciation is produced on the device by the iOS built-in speech synthesis service, and the bundle contains no audio files at all. Studying requires no network.
  • The app's network layer is a local mock implementation containing no real network calls (no HTTP requests, no WebSockets).
  • The interface is rendered in an embedded WKWebView whose content is read from inside the app bundle through a custom local resource scheme. That WebView loads only local content from inside the app bundle — no external URL is ever opened inside the app. http/https links (such as the full text of this privacy policy) are handed to the system browser instead; every other scheme is refused. The app itself provides no way to browse the internet.
  • The app requests no microphone, camera, contacts, calendar, location, photo library or health permissions.

One honest footnote: a default stylesheet shipped with the UI framework (uni-app) contains a decorative background-image reference to cdn.dcloud.net.cn. It is leftover framework boilerplate, carries no user data whatsoever, and simply fails silently when offline with no effect on functionality. We list it because the claim "fully offline" should survive a packet capture.

3. Pronunciation does not need a microphone

The "pronounce / read aloud" feature plays audio; it does not record. Every pronunciation is generated on your device by the iOS built-in speech synthesis (text-to-speech) service; the app bundle contains no pre-recorded audio files. The feature only produces sound and captures no audio input. The app therefore neither requests nor needs microphone access, and contains no pronunciation-scoring or voice-comparison feature.

4. Data stored on your device

The following is kept only in local device storage (the WebView's local storage). It is never uploaded or synced, and the developer has no way to obtain it:

  • Learning progress, studied cards, quiz scores and the mistakes notebook.
  • Bookmarked items.
  • Interface preferences such as a nickname you type in (where that feature is present in this version).

Deleting the app removes all of it.

5. What this app does not do

  • No accounts, no registration, no collection of name, email or phone number.
  • No advertising SDK, analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK; the IDFA is never read.
  • No "Tracking" as defined by Apple's App Privacy framework.
  • No social features, chat, comments or content sharing between users.
  • No ability to open arbitrary web pages.

6. Children's privacy

This app targets vocational English for construction-machinery professionals. It is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children — it collects identity information from no one.

7. Your rights and how to reach us

The developer holds none of your data, so there is no server-side record to access or delete; deleting the app erases everything locally. For any question, write to sntwet@gmail.com.
